About

I am a PhD student in Philosophy of Science at the University of Cambridge. I work on topics in the philosophy of medicine and psychiatry, and bioethics, with a particular interest in addiction, biological functions, and agency and moral responsibility.

My thesis examines how we should understand the concepts of health and disease to better capture the nature of psychiatric conditions and risk factors.

Before Cambridge I completed an MSc in Philosophy and Public Policy at LSE and an undergraduate degree in Philosophy and Economics at The College of William & Mary.
